    How to write a song

    1. Start with Title:, Key: (where Sa is), Tempo: and Time: (4/4, 3/4 or 6/8).
    2. Write a line of notes. Under it, write the words. Leave a blank line before the next phrase.
    3. Notes: Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni or C D E F G A B.
      Flat (komal): re ga dha ni or Db Eb Ab Bb. Tivra Ma: ma or F#.
    4. Higher octave: Sa' / C'. Lower: ,Pa or Pa, / G,
    5. Long or short: Sa=1 beat, Sa-=2, Sa--=3, Sa_=½, Sa.=1½, Sa*5=5. Rest: ~
    6. Chords in brackets, right before the note they start on: [C] [Am] [G7] or [Pa] [Dham].
    7. Words: split syllables with a hyphen, like Twin-kle. Use _ to stretch a word over one more note.
    8. | is a bar line (optional).

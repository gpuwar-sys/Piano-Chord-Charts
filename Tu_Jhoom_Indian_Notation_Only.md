# Tu Jhoom – Only Indian Notation (No C, D, E)

**Song:** Tu Jhoom (Abida Parveen & Naseebo Lal)

Everything below uses only **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni**. No Western note names.

---

## The 7 main notes (shuddha swara)

On the keyboard, find **Sa** first. Then the next keys (going right) are:

**Sa → Re → Ga → Ma → Pa → Dha → Ni → Sa'** (next Sa, higher)

- **Sa** = your starting note (the “home” key)
- **Re** = one key right of Sa  
- **Ga** = one key right of Re  
- **Ma** = one key right of Ga  
- **Pa** = one key right of Ma  
- **Dha** = one key right of Pa  
- **Ni** = one key right of Dha  
- **Sa'** = one key right of Ni (same as Sa, higher)

---

## Version 1 – Sa on the “middle” white key (all white keys)

Put **Sa** on the **white key** that is roughly in the **middle** of the keyboard (many people use this as Sa).  
Then **all 7 notes** (Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni) are **white keys only** – no black keys.

### Chords for Tu Jhoom (Version 1)

Play **4 beats** each, then repeat: **1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 …**

| Step | Left hand (bass) | Right hand (chord – 3 keys together) |
|------|------------------|--------------------------------------|
| **1** | **Dha** | **Dha – Sa – Ga** (same Dha, then Sa and Ga in higher octave) |
| **2** | **Ma**  | **Ma – Dha – Sa** |
| **3** | **Sa**  | **Sa – Ga – Pa** |
| **4** | **Pa**  | **Pa – Ni – Re** |

So the **pattern** is: **Dha, Ma, Sa, Pa** (bass), with the right hand playing the chord notes above.

### Melody: “Tu jhoom jhoom jhoom jhoom”

**Pa Pa Pa Pa** | **Dha Dha Pa** | **Ma Ma Ga** | **Re Sa**

Play this with the right hand; left hand keeps doing **Dha, Ma, Sa, Pa** as above.

---

## Version 2 – Sa one step higher (one black key in the scale)

Put **Sa** on the **next white key** to the right of where you had Sa in Version 1.  
So Sa is **one key higher** than in Version 1.

- **Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa** = white keys (same as before, but shifted right by one key).
- **Dha** = **black key** (the one just to the **left** of the next white key that would be “upper” Ni).
- **Ni** = white key (the one after that black key).

So in Version 2, **only Dha is a black key**; the rest are white.

### Chords for Tu Jhoom (Version 2)

Same **pattern** as Version 1, but the **keys** are different because Sa has moved:

| Step | Left hand (bass) | Right hand (chord) |
|------|------------------|--------------------|
| **1** | **Sa**  | **Sa – Ma – Dha** |
| **2** | **Dha** (black key) | **Dha – Re – Ma** |
| **3** | **Ma**  | **Ma – Dha – Sa** |
| **4** | **Sa** (lower Sa) | **Sa – Ga – Pa** |

So the **pattern** is: **Sa, Dha, Ma, Sa** (bass). Step 2 uses the **black key** for Dha.

### Melody: “Tu jhoom jhoom jhoom jhoom”

**Same swaras:** **Pa Pa Pa Pa** | **Dha Dha Pa** | **Ma Ma Ga** | **Re Sa**

Only the **physical keys** change: Pa, Dha, Ma, Ga, Re, Sa are now on the keys that belong to this new Sa.

---

## What “switching to Dm version” means

- **Version 1** = Sa on the “middle” white key. All white keys. Chords: **Dha, Ma, Sa, Pa** (bass).  
- **Version 2** = Sa one key higher. One black key (Dha). Chords: **Sa, Dha, Ma, Sa** (bass).

“Dm version” on the keyboard = **Version 2**: same **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni** and same **Tu jhoom** melody, but you **move Sa** to the next white key and use the **black key for Dha** when the chord says Dha. It often sits better with the recording.

---

## Summary

- **Chords:** 4 steps, 4 beats each, repeat.  
  - Version 1: bass **Dha – Ma – Sa – Pa**.  
  - Version 2: bass **Sa – Dha – Ma – Sa** (Dha = black key).
- **Melody (Tu jhoom):** **Pa Pa Pa Pa | Dha Dha Pa | Ma Ma Ga | Re Sa** in both versions.
- **Tempo:** Slow (~65); hold each chord and let it ring.

No C, D, E needed – only **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni** and, in Version 2, **one black key for Dha**.

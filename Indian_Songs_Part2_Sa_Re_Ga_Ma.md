**For personal use only – learning at home. Not for distribution or commercial use.**

---

## How to Read This

- **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa** = Indian classical notation (swaras)
- **Sa** = C, **Re** = D, **Ga** = E, **Ma** = F, **Pa** = G, **Dha** = A, **Ni** = B, **Sa'** = C (higher octave)
- Start with **Sa = C** (middle C on keyboard)
- Each swara = 1-2 beats (play smoothly)

## 1. Aye Mere Watan Ke Logon (Patriotic - Simplified)

**Scale: C major (Sa = C)**  
**Tempo: Slow, emotional**

### Main melody

**Sa Re Ga Ma Pa** | **Dha Ni Sa'** | **Sa' Ni Dha Pa** | **Ma Ga Re Sa**

**Pa Dha Ni Sa'** | **Sa' Ni Dha Pa** | **Ma Ga Re Sa** | **Re Ga Ma Pa**

**Sa Re Ga Ma Pa** | **Ma Ga Re Sa** | **Pa Dha Ni Sa'** | **Sa' Ni Dha Pa Ma**

*Play each group smoothly. Very emotional and beautiful song.*

## Practice Tips

1. **Start slow** - Master the pattern before speeding up
2. **Say swara names** - Helps with memory and rhythm
3. **Use smooth transitions** - Don't jump between notes abruptly
4. **Practice one song** until comfortable before moving to next
5. **Sing along** - Helps with timing and expression
6. **Count beats** - Each group of swaras = 4 beats (unless noted)

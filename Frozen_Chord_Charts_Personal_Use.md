# Frozen Songs – Simple Chord Charts (Piano Notes Included)
**For personal use only – learning at home with a child. Not for distribution or commercial use.**

---

## How to Read This

- **Chord name** (e.g. **Am**) = play the notes listed under it.
- **Left hand (LH)** = play these keys with your left hand (often one note or a simple bass).
- **Right hand (RH)** = play these three notes together (the chord) with your right hand.
- You can sing or hum the melody while playing the chords.

---

## Piano Note Guide (Middle of Piano)

| Chord | Left hand (bass) | Right hand (chord notes) |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Am** | A (below middle C) | A – C – E |
| **F**  | F                 | F – A – C |
| **C**  | C                 | C – E – G |
| **G**  | G                 | G – B – D |
| **Dm** | D                 | D – F – A |
| **Em** | E                 | E – G – B |
| **Bb** | Bb (B flat)       | Bb – D – F |

*Tip: Find "middle C" on the piano (usually near a lock or brand name), then count up and down for these letter notes. Bb (B flat) is the black key just to the left of B.*

## 1. Let It Go

**Key: A minor**  
**Suggested tempo: slow and steady (you can count 1-2-3-4 per chord).**

### Chorus (the famous “Let it go…” part)

| Chord | Count (hold each 4 beats) | LH note | RH notes |
|-------|---------------------------|---------|----------|
| **Am** | 1 2 3 4 | A | A – C – E |
| **F**  | 1 2 3 4 | F | F – A – C |
| **C**  | 1 2 3 4 | C | C – E – G |
| **G**  | 1 2 3 4 | G | G – B – D |

**Repeat:** Am → F → C → G over and over while singing the chorus.

### Verse (optional – “The snow glows white…”)

Same four chords in order: **Am – F – C – G**. Repeat as needed.

### Playing notes (melody hint for “Let it go…”)

- On **Am**: melody often starts around **E** or **A** (right hand).
- On **F**: try **A** or **C**.
- On **C**: try **E** or **G**.
- On **G**: try **G** or **B**.

Let the child sing the tune and match one or two of these notes to get the sound.

## 2. Do You Want to Build a Snowman?

**Key: C major**  
**Tempo: bouncy, not too fast.**

### Main section

| Chord | Count | LH note | RH notes |
|-------|-------|---------|----------|
| **C**  | 1 2 3 4 | C | C – E – G |
| **G**  | 1 2 3 4 | G | G – B – D |
| **Am** | 1 2 3 4 | A | A – C – E |
| **F**  | 1 2 3 4 | F | F – A – C |

**Order:** C → G → Am → F, then repeat.

### “Do you want to build a snowman?” – melody hint

- **C**: often **E** or **G**.
- **G**: often **G** or **D**.
- **Am**: often **C** or **E**.
- **F**: often **A** or **F**.

## 4. Take a Bow (Madonna)

**Key: D minor**  
**Tempo: slow, emotional ballad (hold each chord for 4 beats).**

### Main progression (verse and chorus)

| Chord | Count | LH note | RH notes |
|-------|-------|---------|----------|
| **Dm** | 1 2 3 4 | D | D – F – A |
| **Bb** | 1 2 3 4 | Bb | Bb – D – F |
| **F**  | 1 2 3 4 | F | F – A – C |
| **C**  | 1 2 3 4 | C | C – E – G |

**Order:** Dm → Bb → F → C, then repeat throughout the song.

### Playing notes (melody hint for "Take a bow...")

- On **Dm**: melody often starts around **F** or **A** (right hand).
- On **Bb**: try **D** or **F**.
- On **F**: try **A** or **C**.
- On **C**: try **E** or **G**.

*Note: Bb (B flat) is the black key immediately to the left of B. In the left hand, play Bb one octave below middle C.*

## Tips for Playing With a Child

1. **Start with one song** (e.g. “Let It Go” chorus) and one chord at a time.
2. **Say the chord names out loud** while changing (e.g. “Am… F… C… G…”).
3. **Left hand only first**: play just the LH bass note in time, then add RH chords when ready.
4. **Sing along** – the chords will fit the melody.
5. **Go slow** – 4 beats per chord is easier than 2 when starting.

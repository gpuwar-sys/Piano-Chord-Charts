# Indian Songs – Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Notation
**For personal use only – learning at home. Not for distribution or commercial use.**

---

## How to Read This

- **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa** = Indian classical notation (swaras)
- **Sa** = Do (C), **Re** = Re (D), **Ga** = Mi (E), **Ma** = Fa (F), **Pa** = So (G), **Dha** = La (A), **Ni** = Ti (B), **Sa** (higher) = Do (C)
- **Lowercase** (re, ga, ma, dha, ni) = flat notes (komal swaras) - black keys
- **Uppercase** (Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha, Ni) = natural notes (shuddha swaras) - white keys
- **'** (apostrophe) = higher octave (e.g., **Sa'** = higher Sa)
- **,** (comma) = lower octave (e.g., **,Sa** = lower Sa)

### Keyboard Mapping (C scale - Sa = C)

| Swara | Western Note | Key Color | Position |
|-------|--------------|-----------|----------|
| **Sa** | C | White | Base note |
| **Re** | D | White | Next white key |
| **Ga** | E | White | Next white key |
| **Ma** | F | White | Next white key |
| **Pa** | G | White | Next white key |
| **Dha** | A | White | Next white key |
| **Ni** | B | White | Next white key |
| **Sa'** | C (higher) | White | Octave higher |

*Tip: Start with Sa = C (middle C). All these songs use the natural scale (shuddha swaras) unless noted.*

## 1. Jana Gana Mana (National Anthem)

**Scale: C major (Sa = C)**  
**Tempo: Slow, dignified**

### Main melody (simplified)

**Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa'**  
**Sa' Ni Dha Pa Ma Ga Re Sa**

**Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa'**  
**Sa' Ni Dha Pa Ma Ga Re Sa**

*Play each swara smoothly, hold for 2 beats. Repeat the pattern.*

### Full pattern (one verse)

**Sa Re Ga Ma** | **Pa Dha Ni Sa'** | **Sa' Ni Dha Pa** | **Ma Ga Re Sa**

**Sa Re Ga Ma** | **Pa Dha Ni Sa'** | **Sa' Ni Dha Pa** | **Ma Ga Re Sa**

## Practice Tips

1. **Start with Sa = C** (middle C on keyboard)
2. **Play slowly** - say each swara name out loud as you play
3. **Use one finger at a time** initially, then try smooth transitions
4. **Practice one song** until comfortable, then move to next
5. **Sing along** - it helps with rhythm and pitch
6. **Count beats** - each swara can be 1-2 beats depending on the song

## Advanced: Adding Rhythm (Taal)

Once comfortable with notes, try adding rhythm:

- **Ta** = 1 beat (play note)
- **Rest** = pause (don't play)
- **Ta Ta** = 2 beats (play note twice or hold)

Example: **Sa Ta Ta** | **Re Ta Ta** | **Ga Ta** | **Ma Ta**

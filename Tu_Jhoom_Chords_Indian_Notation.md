# Tu Jhoom – Chord Chart (Indian Notation + Keyboard)
**Song by Abida Parveen & Naseebo Lal**  
**For personal use only – learning at home. Not for distribution or commercial use.**

---

## How to Use This

- **Chords** = play with left hand (bass) + right hand (chord notes) on keyboard.
- **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa** = melody line in Indian notation (Sa = C in this chart).
- **Tu jhoom jhoom** = the famous hook; chords and melody hints are given below.

---

## Key & Scale

- **Key:** A minor (Indian: **Sa** = A, or use **Sa** = C and play in C minor feel – see note below)
- **Simpler option:** Use **Sa = C** and play in **C minor** (same pattern, easier on keyboard).

### If Sa = C (C minor scale on keyboard)

| Chord | Left hand (bass) | Right hand (chord notes) |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------------|
| **Am** | A | A – C – E |
| **F**  | F | F – A – C |
| **C**  | C | C – E – G |
| **G**  | G | G – B – D |
| **Dm** | D | D – F – A |
| **Em** | E | E – G – B |

*Tip: "Tu Jhoom" often uses a minor, Sufi feel. **Am – F – C – G** works well for the main sections.*

## Main Chord Progression (Verse & Chorus)

Use this pattern through most of the song (4 beats per chord):

| Chord | Count | LH note | RH notes |
|-------|-------|---------|----------|
| **Am** | 1 2 3 4 | A | A – C – E |
| **F**  | 1 2 3 4 | F | F – A – C |
| **C**  | 1 2 3 4 | C | C – E – G |
| **G**  | 1 2 3 4 | G | G – B – D |

**Order:** **Am → F → C → G** then repeat.

*Use the same progression for lines like:*  
*"Pira nu main sine lavaan…" / "Dukkh vi apne sukh vi apne…" / "Tu jhoom jhoom jhoom jhoom"*

## “Tu jhoom jhoom jhoom jhoom” – Melody in Indian Notation (Sa = C)

Simplified melody for the hook (play with right hand while left hand plays chords):

**Pa Pa Pa Pa** | **Dha Dha Pa** | **Ma Ma Ga** | **Re Sa**

**Pa Pa Pa Pa** | **Dha Dha Pa** | **Ma Ma Ga** | **Re Sa**

*Rough fit with chords:*  
- **Am**: use **Pa** (G) or **Ma** (F)  
- **F**: use **Ma** (F) or **Dha** (A)  
- **C**: use **Sa** (C) or **Pa** (G)  
- **G**: use **Pa** (G) or **Dha** (A)

## Section Guide (Lyrics You Shared)

### 1. Opening (Pira nu main sine lavaan…)

**Chords:** Am – F – C – G (repeat)  
**Feel:** Slow, devotional. Hold each chord 4 beats.

### 2. (Duppan de naal larh larh ke… / Dukkh vi apne sukh vi apne…)

**Chords:** Am – F – C – G  
**Melody hint:** Stay on **Sa Re Ga Ma Pa** (C D E F G); **Pa** and **Ma** fit “Tu jhoom” well.

### 3. Chorus – “Tu jhoom jhoom jhoom jhoom”

**Chords:** Am – F – C – G (one chord per bar, or 2 beats each for more movement)  
**Melody:** **Pa Pa Pa Pa** then **Dha Dha Pa** then **Ma Ma Ga Re Sa** (as above).

### 4. (Main diwani… / Duniya raazi karke kamle… / Sari khushiyan mil jaavan te…)

**Chords:** Same – Am – F – C – G  
**Feel:** Build slightly; you can play chords a bit more rhythmically (e.g. bass on 1, chord on 2–3–4).

### 5. End (Tere bas vich kuch vi nahi… / Tu jhoom jhoom…)

**Chords:** Am – F – C – G, then end on **Am**.  
**Melody:** Repeat **Pa Pa Pa Pa | Dha Dha Pa | Ma Ma Ga | Re Sa** and land on **Sa** (C) or **Pa** (G) on the last **Am**.

## Indian Notation Summary (Sa = C)

- **Sa** = C  
- **Re** = D  
- **Ga** = E  
- **Ma** = F  
- **Pa** = G  
- **Dha** = A  
- **Ni** = B  
- **Sa'** = C (higher)

**“Tu jhoom” phrase in swaras:**  
**Pa Pa Pa Pa | Dha Dha Pa | Ma Ma Ga | Re Sa** (repeat as in the song).

---

## Tips for Playing

1. **Listen to the original** to get the slow, swaying (jhoom) feel.  
2. **Start with only chords** (Am – F – C – G); add the melody once comfortable.  
3. **Sing “Tu jhoom jhoom…”** while playing the **Pa Pa Pa Pa** line.  
4. **Keep tempo slow** – the song breathes; don’t rush.  
5. **End verses/chorus on Am** for a natural Sufi/minor feel.
